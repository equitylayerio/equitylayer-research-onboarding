# EquityLayer: equity research harness for agents

This submission candidate starts from public release `06d3bb568de6847eb4bef71b707d14d6755cff52`.
It does not include the separate local loop-engineering candidate.
The public client, hosted backend, and founder submission materials have separate version and verification boundaries.

## What the user gets

EquityLayer supplies domain research methods and source-linked evidence to the AI an investor already uses.
The initial theme is AI Compute across the US, Taiwan, Japan, and Korea. CPO and packaging are research questions within that supply chain, not separate finance chatbots.
An industry map helps the user choose companies and a question. The MCP interface gives their AI a research method, evidence limits, and a structured result.

[Research setup](research-setup.md) maps a company's evidence needs to the user's declared tool schemas and generates a Codex or Claude Code configuration recipe.
AI Compute and PCB / CCL profiles share the result contract. Missing access remains a gap; a tool match is not verified data coverage.

The user reviews the result in the local dashboard before accepting a baseline.
Their AI performs the research. EquityLayer does not replace it or provide a general memory platform.

## Demonstration path

Select a theme and companies → give the scope to the user's AI → check evidence → save the result → review the next change.

Use the [research client instructions](client.md) and [review guide](hackathon-review.md).
Missing evidence stays visible. This release uses result-file import for the dashboard return path and does not claim automatic background monitoring.
The map's cross-market company universe is wider than the currently verified or purchasable data coverage.

## Agent research purchases

The optional [Solana MCP buyer](../tools/x402-mcp-bridge/README.md) supports one fixed Devnet USDC research purchase with separate spending approval.
Two retained purchases have published verification: [direct buyer](2026-10-03-devnet-payment-verification.md) and [SDK MCP session](2026-10-03-mcp-purchase-verification.md).
They demonstrate delivery of a research update, not a mainnet sale, commercial demand, or an investment order.
The seller backend remains private and needs a configured local environment for a new purchase.

The proposed first research unit is **AI Compute → Taiwan Monthly Revenue Update**.
Its tracked monthly-revenue scope is 2383.TW, 3037.TW, and 8046.TW. That is one evidence slice, not the full thesis or company universe.
Total issuer revenue cannot establish AI revenue, product mix, or customer allocation by itself.

## Trading boundary

Company research can lead to an external Jupiter handoff. Robinhood Chain evidence and a broker MCP handoff are separate capabilities.
The [trading connection guide](trading-connections.md) describes their verified scope.
Neither the research client nor the evidence tool submits an investment order. No exchange commission or referral revenue is claimed.

## Outside this submission candidate

Internal method scorecards, paired evaluations, model calibration, automatic method promotion, and unfinished persistent monitoring are not this public product's features.
They are development tools. They must not become setup requirements for an ordinary investor or agent.
Private thesis content, source archives, seller storage, credentials, and the private backend are not included.
